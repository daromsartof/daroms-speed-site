# Legal and privacy (GDPR, Swiss nLPD)

> A legal notice and a privacy page before launch; cookieless analytics by default so no consent banner is needed.

- Legal notice: owner, address, registration number, host (Cloudflare/Vercel/self), contact.
- Privacy: what is collected (forms, analytics), purpose, retention, processors, rights and contact.
- A consent banner only if a tracker sets cookies or fingerprints (then block it until consent).
