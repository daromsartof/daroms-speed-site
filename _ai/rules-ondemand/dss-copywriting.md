# Copywriting

> Voice from site.spec.md; specific, verifiable, plain; one idea per sentence; no generated-copy tells.

- Method per section: who it is for, the concrete outcome, one proof (number, name, place, date), the next step.
- Headlines: the outcome in the reader's words, under ten words; no puns unless the brand is playful.
- Replace adjectives with facts ("forty tables a year" not "high-quality furniture").
- Rhythm: vary sentence length; avoid triads of adjectives, rhetorical questions, "not just X, it's Y".
- Never invent testimonials, clients, figures or awards: leave the placeholder and list it for the user.
- Calls to action name the action ("Book a fitting"), never "Learn more" twice on a page.
