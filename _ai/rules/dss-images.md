# Images and media

> Media (next/image) with a real alt, a ratio and sizes; priority only on the LCP image; no placeholder left at launch.

- Real photos beat stock; stock must match the DNA imagery direction. `public/placeholders/*` are flagged by `dss doctor`.
- Local images under public/ (AVIF/WebP generated by next/image). Remote hosts need `images.remotePatterns` in the manifest.
- SVG: `unoptimized` (Media does it for .svg). Decorative images: `alt=""`.
- Video: muted, playsInline, poster, no autoplay with sound; embeds through CSP presets (youtube, vimeo).
